CrowPSX

PS1-style rendering for Unity · on the Asset Store

A shader suite that recreates how PS1 games looked: vertex snapping, affine texture warping and low color depth. It covers regular surfaces, vertex-painted materials, terrain, foliage, particles, decals and the sky, so a whole scene can share one style. It also has a batch material manager, and snapping that stays stable in VR.

CrowSave

A save and load system for Unity. Each object saves and restores its own data, keyed by scene and ID, so two copies of a level can't overwrite each other. Destroyed objects stay destroyed after loading, unchanged objects are skipped when saving, and there's a small ring of in-memory checkpoints for rewinding.

FlashGuard

An overlay for people sensitive to flashing light. It copies the desktop every frame, finds regions that flicker at risky rates on the GPU, and limits how fast those regions can change on screen. Normal motion is left alone. It's experimental and not medically validated, and the README says so up front.
